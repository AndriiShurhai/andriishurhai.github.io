Arena-combat prototype built around recording player input and replaying both runs simultaneously in battle. Refactored the gameplay core away from Unity's MonoBehaviour loop into an isolated simulation layer.

  • Tick-indexed deterministic input recording and replay playback.
  • Swept collision checks, projectile deflections, and damage calculations.

About

I'm a final-year Computer Science student at Bohdan Khmelnytsky National University of Cherkasy, Ukraine, focused on gameplay programming with Unity and C#.

I focus on game systems and architecture: decoupling gameplay rules from the rendering layer, building deterministic mechanics, and writing tests to keep systems reliable.

Outside of game dev, I do competitive programming — I've participated in ICPC SEERC twice (2024 and 2025) and develop desktop tools with C#/.NET alongside my Unity projects.

I'm looking for a junior Unity or gameplay programmer role where I can work on real systems and keep improving with a team.
